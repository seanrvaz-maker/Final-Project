About the Solar System

Our solar system is made up of the Sun and everything that travels around it, including eight planets, moons, asteroids, comets, and other objects.

The four planets closest to the Sun are Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars. These are rocky planets. Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune are much larger outer planets made mostly of gases and ices.
